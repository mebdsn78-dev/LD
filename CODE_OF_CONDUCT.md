# Code of Conduct

This project is open to everyone in the world. To keep it a good place to work together:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners and non-native English/Arabic speakers.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive. No spam or advertising.
- Don't share other people's private information.
- Use the project only for **defensive** purposes. Contributions meant to harm users (malware, spying, evasion of the tool's own protections) are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.

يُرجى الاحترام والصبر وحسن الظن، وعدم الإساءة لأي شخص، واستخدام المشروع للأغراض الدفاعية فقط.
